# International lanes review (Nov 12 2026)

Every lane that bets on national-team football was replayed on Kalshi's own international history, with the form rules recomputed from results and the filters written down first. No lane changes. Nothing was promoted or pulled.

"Points" is the share of bets won minus the price paid. Returns are per bet after fees, on honest fills.

## Timing: does betting days early cost anything?

Not on the lanes' own bets.

| Lane | Bets | Logged (median hours early) | Price when logged | Price 3h before kickoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 3.5 low scoring | 26 | 75 | 77.5c | 77.6c |
| Team 1 form | 8 | 47 | 82.1c | 82.9c |
| Over 1.5 ranked | 17 | 56 | 75.9c | 77.1c |

Early books are wide in general. Three days out the average gap between bid and ask on a totals market is 28.7c, and only 58% of books are within 5c. Three hours out it is 2.9c and 95%. On every match, an early ask is about 4c dearer than the ask near kickoff.

The lanes are protected because the tracker only bets when the book is tight. That rule should stay.

These are form rules: the matches are chosen from results, not from the price. So timing changes only the price paid, not which matches are picked. That is why the Eredivisie problem does not repeat here.

## Price

Olu's question: do prices matter in these lanes? The bands were fixed before looking: under 70c, 70-80c, 80-90c, 90c and over. Entry three hours before kickoff.

**1. Inside the rules, the win rate is about the same at every price, so the cheaper picks pay most.**

| Under 3.5 low scoring (Nations League and friendlies) | Bets | Won | Price | Return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 70c | 6 | 83.3% | 58.5c | +41.1% |
| 70-80c | 9 | 100% | 75.8c | +29.8% |
| 80-90c | 20 | 90.0% | 83.2c | +7.0% |
| 90c and over | 3 | 100% | 92.3c | +7.8% |

- Under 80c: 14 wins in 15, +34%. At 80c and over: 21 in 23, +7%.
- It shows in both periods (under 80c: 5 of 6, then 9 of 9), but the bands are too small to pass the keep rule. It is a watch, not a version 2.
- Team 1 form is the same shape: 9 of 9 at 90c and over for +6%; 10 of 11 under 90c for about +22%.

**2. The same rules do not work on club matches.**

| Rule | Internationals | Clubs (the lane's own club bets) |
|---|---|---|
| Under 3.5 low scoring | 92.1% at 78.3c, +17.8% (38) | 71.8% at 71.6c, −0.3% (39) |
| Team 1 form | 95.0% at 82.7c, +14.8% (20) | 79.4% at 83.4c, −6.6% (34) |

**3. The market itself prices internationals differently from clubs at the same band.**

| Under 3.5, every match | Internationals | Clubs |
|---|---|---|
| Under 70c | +4.0 points (169) | −3.2 points (725) |
| 70-80c | +1.1 (146) | +0.1 (238) |
| 80-90c | −5.2 (81) | +0.4 (29) |

- A cheap international under wins more often than its price; a cheap club under wins less often. A dear international under is overpriced.
- Side to score, every match: internationals are close to fair in every band. Clubs at 80-90c are 5.6 points overpriced on 446 (score −3.3).
- None of the international gaps reaches a score of 2 on its own.

**4. The price move before kickoff is a weak warning, not a rule.**

Measured on the midpoint between the early read and three hours out.

| Under 3.5, every match | Bets | Won | Price at 3h | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price rose 2c or more | 33 | 75.8% | 76.9c | −1.2 |
| Flat | 61 | 80.3% | 73.6c | +6.8 |
| Price fell 2c or more | 65 | 56.9% | 62.1c | −5.2 |

The rule's own picks: both picks whose price fell 2c or more lost; 9 of the other 10 won. Two bets is not evidence. On the side-to-score and over 1.5 markets the move separates nothing.

## African qualifiers

Added after the first pass, which had no results for them. Results for AFCON and its qualifiers come from ESPN's public scoreboard. All 48 qualifier matches Kalshi has settled (Sep 24 to Oct 6) are now in the replay, with form recomputed including those games.

**Live scope.** The live international lanes do not accept African qualifier markets. The tracker lists two Kalshi series for internationals: UEFA Nations League and international friendlies. This window Kalshi has 24 AFCON qualifiers open; the lanes will not bet them. An African team can still be bet in a friendly.

**Verdict: keep them excluded, for every lane.**

| Lane | The rule on African qualifiers | Every match, same set | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 3.5 low scoring | 9 wins in 15 at 81.9c, −28.5% | 70.8% at 77.7c, −7.9% (48) | Exclude. It loses here. |
| Team 1 form | Never fired | 74.7% at 65.0c, +15.6% (91 sides) | Too few to tell. Default: exclude. |
| Team 2+ form | Never fired | 38.0% at 39.8c, −3.8% (92 sides) | Too few to tell. Default: exclude. |
| Over 1.5 ranked | 5 wins in 6 at 75.2c, +7.1% | 77.1% at 69.8c, +8.1% (48) | Too few to tell. Default: exclude. |

- **Under 3.5 low scoring** fires on almost a third of African qualifiers, at a dear price, and lost 6 of 15. With the World Cup (2 of 4) and the Concacaf Nations League (4 of 8), the rule is 15 wins in 27 outside its own two competitions.
- **By price band, under 3.5:** the rule's picks were 1 win in 3 at 70-80c and 8 in 12 at 80-90c. On every African match the 80-90c under won 75.9% on 29, 7.4 points less than its price.
- **The market expects too few goals in these matches.** Unders are 6.9 points overpriced, overs are 7.3 points underpriced, and a side to score is 9.7 points underpriced (score 2.1). That is one international window, so it is a lead to watch on this window's 24 matches, not a rule.
