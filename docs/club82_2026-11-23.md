## The two lanes
1. **club82_u35**: back the match total UNDER 3.5 (buy NO on Kalshi's over 3.5 rung, `KX{EPL,LALIGA,SERIEA,BUNDESLIGA,LIGUE1,UCL}TOTAL`) when EITHER team is at 82% or better for "under 3.5" this season. One entry a game.
2. **club82_tt15**: back a qualifying team to score 2 or more (buy YES on its team-total over 1.5 rung, `KX{...}TEAMTOTAL`) when THAT team is at 82% or better for "scored 2+". One entry per qualifying team (both teams qualifying in one game is two entries, one on each team's market).

## The qualifier (recomputed from the games already played, at every entry read)
- Season 2026-27 (games from 2026-07-01), all competitions combined: Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and Champions League. MLS is out. A team's games count wherever they were played.
- 90 minutes only: ESPN's final score as `data/streaks_raw.json` stores it (`soccer_rules`/`soccer_team_rules` data; extra-time and shoot-out games are not in it).
- At least 8 games played, and hit rate of 0.818 or better (9/11 = 0.8182 counts; 9/12 = 75% does not). Config: `MIN_GAMES`, `THRESHOLD`.
- Under 3.5 is a total of 3 goals or fewer, both teams. Scored 2+ is the team's own goals.
- No look-ahead: only games that kicked off BEFORE the match being read count. A team under the bar stops being bet that game and re-enters when it climbs back; no list is carried between reads.
- "Since" on the page is the date of the game after which the team's current qualifying run began. The 75-81% "watch" teams (8+ games) are shown in a fold.

## The entry
One entry per market, taken 30 to 60 minutes before kickoff (the kickoff-window reads run every half hour from 90 to 20 minutes; a market with no usable quote at one read is tried again at the next read inside 30 to 60).
Under: NO at 1 minus the YES bid. Team over 1.5: YES at the ask. Spread (YES ask minus YES bid) 5 cents or less, price 3 to 97 cents, 7% taker fee (7% x p x (1 - p)). Held to settlement on Kalshi's own result.
Team totals often open late: a match with no event, no rung or no usable quote is logged as a skip row with its reason ("no market", "spread over 5c", "price outside 3-97c") and shown on the page; none is dropped silently.

## The control (per lane)
At the same read as the entry: the under 3.5 (NO) in EVERY game, and the team over 1.5 (YES) for EVERY team in every game, qualifying or not, same fee, held to settlement. The page shows each lane beside its control.

## Reading
Olu reads each lane at 10 settled entries, then at 30, against its control after fees. Paper only; the lane never trades and never changes another lane's record.
