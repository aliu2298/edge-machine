## The question
v1 asks "does the team clear the fixed line at 90% (NBA) / 82% (soccer)?" v2 asks "by how much does it clear it?", and bets a harder Kalshi rung where the cushion supports it.

## The two v2 rules per club (same gate, same read times, same spread limit and 'wide' skip, one entry a game and section)
- **v2_cushion**: among the rungs the game lists and quotes (spread 5 cents or less), a qualifying team's SAFE rung is the hardest one at or past the v1 line that the team still clears at the club rate (NBA 90%, soccer 81.8%) over at least 10 prior games (point in time, games before the night/kickoff). Both teams qualifying: the harder rung. Over: a higher line is harder; under: a lower line is harder. If the v1 gate fails there is no v2 entry.
- **v2_value**: among the rungs a qualifying team clears at the club rate over at least 10 games (any line, harder or easier than v1), the one with the biggest (hit rate - cost), where hit rate is the team's own prior hit rate at that rung and cost is the ask (NO: 1 - YES bid) plus the 7% fee (7% x p x (1 - p)). Only if the value is 5 points or more. Both teams qualifying: the bigger value.
- Price, fee, spread, read windows (NBA 15-40 minutes before the tip; soccer 30-60 minutes before kickoff), VPS stamps and the backfilled marker are v1's. Rule ids: `nba_qlines_v2_<1Q..2H>_<over|under>_<cushion|value>` and `club82_v2_<u35|tt15>_<cushion|value>`; own rows in the same ledgers.
- Each pick stores the actual (total or team goals) and "cleared by" (actual minus the rung, signed for the direction: positive is a win).
- Graded against v1 AND the same blind every-game control (v1's control rows: the v1 line, every game, same read). Read at 10 settled, then 30.
- No opponent filter: Phase 1 found the opponent's cushion correlates with the realised cushion (+0.27 NBA) but does not change return (priced in), so a v2_value-plus-opponent rule is not built.
