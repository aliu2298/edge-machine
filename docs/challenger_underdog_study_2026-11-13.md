# Challenger tennis underdogs: is the cheap side real, and does "won here" add anything? (Nov 13 2026)

Research only. No lane, page or Production change. The periods, the tests and the rules were written down before the results they apply to were read.

Data: every settled Kalshi ATP Challenger match, Jan 12 to Nov 13 2026 (10,818 matches with one-minute prices), and every WTA Challenger match (2,384). Kalshi lists no ITF matches.

- **Found** on Jan 12 to Jun 30 (5,847 matches).
- **Unseen**: Jul 1 to Oct 26 (4,316 matches), read once, with the rules written first.
- **Live window**: Oct 27 to Nov 13 (655 matches), where the lane's page record comes from. Reported, never used to find or confirm.

Bets are YES at the ask, spread 5c or less, one hour before the scheduled start, held to settlement, after fees. "Score" is the return over its error with a calendar day as one outcome; 2 is the bar on found, 1.5 on unseen.

## Verdict

**No profitable underdog strategy. The cheap side loses over the year, and "won here" is the worst part of it.**

| | Found (Jan to Jun) | Unseen (Jul to Oct 26) | Live window (Oct 27 on) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blind 15-30c band | −15.3% on 1,394 | −7.0% on 1,098 | +15.7% on 177 |
| "Won here, favourite has not played here" | −30.5% on 186 | **−39.4% on 124** | +24.4% on 36 |
| Rest of the band | −12.9% on 1,208 | −2.9% on 974 | +13.5% on 141 |
| Every player under 40c | −21.1% on 3,541 | −12.3% on 2,756 | −3.2% on 425 |

- **The blind cheap side is not real.** Over the whole history the 15-30c band is −9.8% on 2,669 bets, score −2.9. The live window's gain (score 1.1) is one good stretch after eight losing months in the ten before it.
- **"Won here" fails.** It lost in every month of both periods, and it did worse than the rest of its own band both times. Whole history: −28.0% on 346.
- **No version 2 is justified.** 27 selection reasons, a rating built from results, and three entry times were tested. Nothing passed.
- **The money is on the other side, and only just**: favourites priced 90-98c made +2.6% then +1.8%. That missed its pass mark narrowly and is a price band, not a selection.

Recommendation, for Olu to decide: retire the "won here" lane. Keep the blind band only as a control.

## 2. Does "already won here" add anything?

No. On the found months, in the 15-30c band:

| Reason | Bets | Return | Rest of the band |
|---|---|---|---|
| Won here, opponent has not played here (the lane) | 186 | −30.5% | −12.9% |
| Won here, any opponent | 752 | −17.7% | −12.4% |
| Opponent has not played here | 795 | −16.9% | −13.1% |
| The mirror: has not played here, opponent won here | 33 | −6.2% | −15.5% |
| Played another tournament in the last 7 days | 302 | −34.1% | −10.1% |
| Opponent played here under 20 hours ago | 222 | −0.2% | −20.7% |

- 27 reasons were tested on the cheap side, the lane's band, the middle and the favourite: 106 cells. None passed. None was even above zero with a gap of 3 points over the rest and a score of 2.
- Also tested: matches played here by each player, round, rest and the length of the last match, a semi-final or final elsewhere the week before, results against the price in the last five matches, and whether the price rose or fell since the open.
- The idea behind the lane (match play on site against a cold favourite) points the wrong way if anything: the market already prices a favourite's first match.

## 3. Ratings against the price

Jeff Sackmann's open results files are no longer public, and tennis-data.co.uk has no Challengers. So ratings were built from Kalshi's own 15,900 ATP and Challenger results, point in time.

- The rating loses to the price: 0.257 against the market's 0.216 (lower is better).
- Where the rating said a cheap player was 10 or more points underpriced: −18.3% on 552.
- Surface, travel, home player, indoor or outdoor and time of day were not tested: there is no clean source for them.

## 4. Entry timing

- **The lane's "one hour before the start" is hindsight.** The start is read from the price path after the match. Kalshi's expected expiration is the scheduled start plus three hours, so there is a clock known in advance. Play begins a median 55 minutes after it (5% within 9 minutes, 5% more than five hours late).
- **The cheap side gets cheaper before the match.** On the same 1,128 matches its ask averaged 29.8c at the open and 27.7c an hour before; it fell 2c or more on half of them. Bought at the open: −23.1%. An hour before: −17.8%. Ten minutes before: −17.5%.
- So the open is the worst time to buy an underdog, and nothing changes in the last hour.
- **A trap in this kind of replay.** 6.2% of matches show no start in the price path at all: favourites winning without a swing. In those, the cheap side won 1 of 270. A replay that drops them flatters underdogs by about 3.5 points. The lane's engine does not drop them; an early version of this study's own columns did, and was corrected.

## 5. The favourite side

The pre-stated question: if cheap players lose, is the favourite cheap? Bought the cheaper way (its own YES, or NO on the opponent), an hour before:

| | Found | Unseen | Live window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Favourite 60c and over | +1.2% on 4,022 | −1.7% on 3,082 | −5.7% on 467 |
| Favourite 90-98c | +2.6% on 447, score 2.1 | +1.8% on 336, score 1.3 | +2.1% on 44 |

- 90-98c was sent to the unseen months as a rule and **missed its mark** (score 1.3 against 1.5). Ten minutes before the start it scores 1.6.
- It is a price band with no selection reason, on a 94c contract. Olu has said those are low value. It is not recommended as a lane.

## Limits

- **One year, one tour level.** Kalshi's Challenger markets start in January 2026.
- **Prices are one-minute quotes; fills were not run on trade prints**, because nothing passed that would need it. In the found months 454 of 1,394 band entries had a trade at the ask within 15 minutes, and those did no better (−22%).
- **The live window is 18 days.** Its +15.7% on the blind band has a score of 1.1.
- **No outside data.** Rankings, surface and travel could not be tested.

## Build spec

For whoever builds. **No new lane and no version 2.**

- **"Challenger underdog who has won here" (`challenger_won_here_hold`):** recommended stage: retired, record kept. Rule, scope and entry unchanged until then. Retiring it is Olu's decision.
- **The blind 15-30c baseline (`challenger_cheap_hold_base`):** keep as a control row only.
- **The one change from version 1:** none.
- **If either stays on the page:** its status line should say it lost over the ten months before it was registered (−28.0% on 346 for the rule, −9.8% on 2,669 for the band).
- **Optional, only if Olu wants a price-band row:** the 90-98c favourite, an hour before the scheduled start (Kalshi's expected expiration minus four hours), bought the cheaper way, held. About 19 bets a week.

### Pre-registration text, if the lane is kept instead of retired

> "Won here", unchanged, Sandbox paper. Read once at 100 settled bets counted from Nov 2 2026.
> - Keep only if its return is above zero AND above the blind 15-30c band on the same days by 10 points or more.
> - Otherwise retire: ten months of history say it does worse than the band.
