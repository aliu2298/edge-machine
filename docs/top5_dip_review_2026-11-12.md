# Top-5 goalless dip review (Nov 12 2026)

Review only. No lane, page or Production change. The splits, the tests and the verdict rule were written down before any new result was read.

Lane `top5_goalless_dip`: on over 1.5 goals in the five big leagues and the Champions League, rest a YES bid at 55% of the pregame price from kickoff; when it fills, rest a sale at the pregame price; hold what does not sell.

"Return" is per fill after fees on honest fills, unless a row says otherwise. "Score" is the return over its error, with a match day counted as one outcome; 2 or more is the bar.

## Verdict

**Keep version 1 as it is. No version 2.**

- The rule was replayed on last season, which it had never seen: **+17.9% a fill on 389 fills, score 3.9.**
- Every period since Nov 2025 is positive except 14 summer qualifier fills.
- No league or price band is better or worse in a way that repeats. The Bundesliga and Ligue 1 losses in the found window were gains last season.
- It is fading. Plan on about +8% a fill, not +16%.

**One correction.** The lane is not live. It went back to the Sandbox on Nov 8 and its feed has listed no match since. So the decision before clubs resume on Nov 20 is whether it goes back, not whether to pull it. That is Olu's call; the facts for it are under "Going live again".

## 1. Where the found window's +16.6% goes

Mostly nowhere. On honest fills it is +12.8%.

| Step, found window | Fills | Return |
|---|---|---|
| The page's figure | 146 | +16.6% |
| Same fills without the resting fee the page charges (the exchange charges none) | 146 | +18.2% |
| Honest fills (a touch is not a fill), sale on a touch | 145 | +15.3% |
| Honest fills, honest sale | 145 | +12.8% |
| The same fills held to the end, no sale | 145 | +4.4% |

- **Fees:** the page is 1.6 points too harsh.
- **Fill rule:** costs 2.9 points. Three fills only touched the bid; each was worth +79% on the page.
- **Sale rule:** costs 2.5 points. Some sales only touched the price.
- **The sale is the lane.** Held to the end the fills make +4.4%: bought at 43.3c, won 44.8%. The dip price itself is about fair.

What the sale does, found window: 65 fills sold in matches that then won, 25 sold in matches that then lost, 55 never sold and lost. The 25 turn a full loss into about +80%. A sale in a losing match happens when one goal comes soon after the fill: needing one more goal with most of a half left is priced near the pregame price.

Last season the same steps read +16.4% (page), +17.9% (honest), +12.5% (held).

### Which fills pay

Fills were split by what the price did in the ten minutes after.

| Fill | Fills | Share | Won v price paid | Return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price kept falling (5c or more lower) | 376 | 66% | 41% v 42.9c | +4.1% |
| Price did not keep falling | 190 | 34% | 57% v 40.9c | +39.6% |

- Two fills in three are the market moving on the match: a long 0-0. Those are fair. They are not a loss.
- The profit is the other third: someone sold through the book for a moment and the price came back. The lane is paid for standing in the book.
- This cannot be a filter. It is only known after the fill.
- Their share is shrinking: 46% and 43% of fills in the first two periods below, 20% and 19% in the last two.

## 4. Is any subset real?

No. Ten cells were tested on the found window: six leagues and four price bands.

- The best cell is Serie A, score 2.1. After correcting for ten tests it does not clear the bar, and shuffling the labels beats it about half the time.
- No cell passes the written test (positive with a score of 2 in the found window, cleared after correction, and positive again last season).
- The two losing leagues of the found window were the two best last season. Dropping them would have been fitting noise.

## 5. What the lane's own record can tell

Very little, for a long time.

- One fill is worth about +80% or −100%, so results swing widely.
- A score of 2 needs about 240 fills if the true return is +16%, and about 600 if it is +10%.
- The lane fills about 2.7 times a match day.
- "Decide at 30" cannot work here: 30 fills drawn from this record show a loss about 1 time in 7.

The replay is the evidence. The record is a check that nothing has broken.

## Going live again

- **Promotion gate, rule 1** (honest return on its own entries above zero): the Sandbox record is +7.7% on 32 fills. It passes.
- **Rule 2** (the evidence test): the lane's own record fails it, with 32 fills over 7 match days. The replay passes all five checks: 566 fills, 187 match days, score 4.2, both halves up, up without its best day. Going live needs Olu's waiver on rule 2, or Olu accepting the replay as the evidence.
- **Size:** the profit is momentary selling through a thin book. A small bid collects it. A larger one would not collect in proportion, so this stays a small-stake lane.
- **If it goes back,** it should not be judged on fewer than about 100 fills.

## Limits

- **One earlier season.** Kalshi's soccer totals start in Nov 2025.
- **Fading.** The older half of all fills made +27.0%, the newer half +5.2%.
- **Kickoff times.** Kalshi's archived markets carry no kickoff, so last season's kickoffs are ESPN's. 34 matches could not be matched and are left out.
- **Thin books last season.** 348 of 1,257 matches had a pregame price that failed the lane's own trade check and are left out, as the lane itself would.
- **Summer qualifiers** lost on 14 fills. They are inside the lane's scope and too few to act on.

## Build spec

For whoever builds. **Nothing needs building for the rule: version 1 is kept unchanged.**

- **Rule:** over 1.5 goals. Pregame price P is the YES ask an hour before kickoff, spread 5c or less, P 10c to 95c, checked against trades. A resting YES bid at 55% of P from kickoff. On a fill, a resting sale at P. What does not sell is held to settlement.
- **Scope:** Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Champions League. All six stay.
- **Entry timing:** unchanged.
- **The one change from version 1:** none.
- **Retire or keep:** keep, Sandbox paper, on its record from Oct 31.
- **Optional, words only:** the lane's status line says "review at 10, decide at 30". "Review at 100 fills, decide at 240" matches what the numbers can show.
- **Only if Olu sends it back live:** the feed is switched off by `ENDED_ON` in `top5_dip_production.py`. Turning it back on is a Production move and needs Olu's go and the gate waiver recorded.
