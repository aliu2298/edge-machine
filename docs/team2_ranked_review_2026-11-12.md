# Team 2+ ranked review (Nov 12 2026)

Review only. No lane, page or Production change. The splits, the tests and the verdict rule were written down before any new result was read.

The rule: each day, back the favourite to score 2+ where Kalshi's ask sits furthest below a fixed table of how often a favourite of that win price scores twice. Top 2 a day, 5 points of margin or more, and the underdog must have conceded 2+ in at least 4 of its last 10.

"Points" is the share of bets won minus the price paid. "Score" is the result over its error, with a match day counted as one outcome; 2 or more is the bar.

- **Its own record,** +20.4% on 27, is well short of the bar.

## 2. The premise, on bookmaker history

Five leagues (Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1), 2013/14 to 2025/26. For each match's favourite, the fair chance of scoring 2+ was worked out from the closing match odds and the over/under 2.5 line. The rule's margin is its table minus that fair chance; the day's top 2 at 5 points or more are its picks.

| Favourites | Teams | Scored 2+ | Fair chance | The rule's table |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Every favourite, 2013/14 to 2020/21 | 14,507 | 51.3% | 51.1% | 53.3% |
| The rule's picks, 2013/14 to 2020/21 | 1,866 | 43.7% | 45.1% | 53.8% |
| Every favourite, 2021/22 to 2025/26 | 8,908 | 52.5% | 51.4% | 52.7% |
| The rule's picks, 2021/22 to 2025/26 | 1,211 | 43.4% | 42.2% | 51.3% |

- **Against the fair chance the picks are level:** −1.4 points, then +1.2. Neither is close to the bar.
- **Against the table they fall short by 10.1 points, then 7.8.** Both far past the bar, in the wrong direction.
- **The margin is the match total.** The picks' matches expect 2.4 goals; all matches expect 2.8. Across every favourite, a bigger margin goes with a lower expected total (correlation −0.70).

So when a favourite's 2+ price looks cheap against the table, it is because the match is expected to be low-scoring. The price is right and the table is wrong.

## 4. Is any subset real?

No. Thirteen cells on the rule's Kalshi picks: competition, the favourite's win price, the size of the margin, the entry time.

- Nothing clears the correction. The best cell, favourites at 0.70 and over, has a score of 1.6 on 11 bets; shuffled labels beat it 1 time in 4.
- A bigger margin did worse: with both entry times together, 8 points and over returned −21.7% on 25, against +12.2% on 58 at 5 to under 8. That fits section 2: the biggest margins are the lowest-scoring matches.
- Entry time: a day before against an hour before shows no difference that could be told from chance.

## 5. What the record can tell

## Limits

- **Bookmaker history is league matches in five leagues.** No European cups, no internationals.
- **The Kalshi replay is 13 weeks.** Kalshi lists team totals only from August 2026.
- **The underdog filter in the replay** could name the underdog in 580 of 659 matches; where it could not, the match is kept, as the lane does.
- **Internationals could not be tested** on any history. The fade there has no settled bet.
