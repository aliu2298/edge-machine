# Crypto lanes review (Nov 12 2026)

The live crypto lane was replayed on Kalshi's daily 17:00 ET coin ladders since June 1 (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, HYPE; 819 coin-closes), with the filters written down first. No lane changes. Nothing was promoted or pulled.

"Points" is the share of bets won minus the price paid. Returns are per bet after fees, on honest fills.

| Lane | Stage | Verdict |

- 131 bets on 107 days; 111 of them confirmed by trade prints.

- Entry timing is fine: the lane buys 2 to 3.5 hours before the close, which is what the research used.

## Filters tried

A filter had to beat version 1 by 2 points on the never-seen period and again on the period after, with a score of 2 or more. The period after has only 18 bets, so nothing could reach it.

| Quiet v busy day | +16.6 v −2.4 | +16.5 v +2.9 | +14.5 v +8.6 | Watch |
| Rung price rose 3c or more | +10.1 v +2.1 | +1.7 v +20.4 | +11.1 v +21.5 | No |

**The two watches.** Same direction in all three periods, but neither reaches the bar.

- **Weekend closes:** 30 wins in 31, +25.6%. Weekdays: 82.0%, +7.0%.
- **Quiet days** (the rung's own price moved little in the five hours before entry): 92.2% on 77, +19.3%. Busy days: 75.9% on 54, +0.1%.
- Weekday and busy together is where the lane gives it back: 72.9% on 48, −3.6%.
- Neither shows on the YES twin, so this is not a general rule about quiet markets.

## Limits

- The replay takes one hourly quote three hours before the close with a spread of 3c or less. The lane reads the live book up to three times and needs 25 contracts on offer. They are close but not the same.
- Before September the alt-coin ladders rarely had a tight book: 15 alt bets in ten weeks.
- A day is one outcome for a coin, but the five coins move together.
