# MLS fade home and the La Liga pair: review (Nov 12 2026)

These three lanes were found on bookmaker closing prices and log their bets three to four days before kickoff. Each was checked on the long bookmaker history and replayed on Kalshi's own prices at three entry times. No lane changes. Nothing was promoted or pulled.

"Points" is the share of bets won minus the price paid. Returns are per bet after fees, on honest fills.

## Verdicts

| Lane | Stage | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| MLS fade home | Live | **Its edge shows only in the seasons it was found on.** Expect about break-even. Recommend moving it back to the Sandbox. |
| La Liga BTTS even | Sandbox | **Retire.** On Kalshi's prices it is fair at every entry time. |
| La Liga under 1.5 underdog | Sandbox | **Too few to tell.** If kept, bet inside 24 hours of kickoff: the early price is 3c dearer on a 12c bet. Not a candidate to go live. |

No filter held for any of the three. Early entry is not what hurts MLS fade home; it does hurt the under 1.5 lane.

## MLS fade home

The rule: NO on the home side when its win price, with the margin removed, is 0.40 to 0.45.

### Fifteen seasons of closing prices

6,326 MLS matches, 1,004 in the band. The rule was measured on 2024 to 2026, so 2012 to 2023 had never been looked at.

| Seasons | Matches | Home side fails to win | Priced | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2012 to 2018 (never seen) | 399 | 53.9% | 57.4% | −3.6 |
| 2019 to 2023 (never seen) | 357 | 57.4% | 57.6% | −0.1 |
| 2024 to 2026 (found here) | 248 | 66.9% | 57.4% | +9.5 |

By season the last three read +16.4, +0.4 and +11.7. After the rule was registered on Sep 27: +7.9 points on 26.

### Why it is not only luck, and why the band overstates it

MLS home sides as a whole have drifted from underpriced to overpriced.

| Home price | 2012 to 2018 | 2019 to 2023 | 2024 to 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.35-0.40 | −2.7 | +3.3 | +2.7 |
| **0.40-0.45 (the lane)** | **−3.6** | **−0.1** | **+9.5** |
| 0.45-0.50 | −3.2 | −1.0 | +1.8 |
| Every match | −3.3 (2,527) | −0.6 (2,227) | +2.8 (1,572) |

The drift is real and worth about 3 points. The lane's band is the best of seven in the recent seasons; its neighbours are at +2.7 and +1.8. A 3-point edge is roughly break-even after fees.

### Filters

Price half, season half, midweek against weekend, and a tight match, on the bookmaker history; the home price falling in the last day, on Kalshi. None was a candidate on 2012 to 2018, so none could hold. Midweek looked strong in 2024 to 2026 (+23.6 points on 42) but was −8.4 and +3.8 in the two earlier eras.

## La Liga BTTS even

The rule: YES on both teams to score when the favourite's price is 0.470 to 0.590, at an ask of 0.54 or less.

- **Bookmaker history, results only:** both teams scored in 53.4%, 55.6% and 55.9% of band matches across the three eras (805, 545 and 34 this season). The rate is steady.
- **Kalshi's prices, 383 matches since November 2025:**

| Entry before kickoff | Bets | Won | Price | Return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 94 hours (where the lane logs) | 36 | 44.4% | 50.3c | −13.1% |
| 24 hours | 51 | 49.0% | 49.5c | −4.2% |
| 3 hours | 47 | 51.1% | 49.5c | −0.2% |

- The steady 55% is already in Kalshi's price: across every match in the band the ask averages 55.2c and both teams scored 55.6% of the time (99 matches).
- The lane's own record is 3 wins in 7.

## La Liga under 1.5 underdog

The rule: NO on over 1.5 goals when the underdog's price is under 0.135, at a NO ask of 0.20 or less.

- **Bookmaker history, results only:** under 1.5 goals in 18.9%, 25.8% and 16.7% of band matches across the three eras.
- **Kalshi's prices:** almost all of its picks are from this season.

| Entry before kickoff | Bets | Won | Price | Return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 94 hours (where the lane logs) | 18 | 11.1% | 13.6c | −24.8% |
| 24 hours | 21 | 19.0% | 12.2c | +36.7% |
| 3 hours | 21 | 14.3% | 12.2c | +6.1% |

- Three or four wins in about twenty bets cannot settle anything.
- On the same 20 matches the NO ask was 15.4c four days out and 12.1c three hours out. That 3c is a quarter of the stake.
- The lane's own record is 3 wins in 9 at 13.9c.

## A lead, not on the menu

In the most balanced La Liga matches (favourite under 0.47) both teams scored 59.2% of the time against an ask of 52.6c, on 174 matches. It was not written down in advance, so it is a lead to test, not a finding.
