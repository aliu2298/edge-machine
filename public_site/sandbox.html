How to read this · what the columns mean
Record is settled bets won–lost on the US exchanges, flat $100 a bet at the price available before the start. Won v priced is wins against the wins the prices implied — beating that is the whole test, and the ranking sorts on it per bet rather than on ROI, which mostly reflects the prices a pair happened to be offered. Verdict is one ladder on every page. Under 10 settled a pair is Too early and is not ranked at all. From 10 to 30 it is Collecting n/30, with its lean so far: Promising or Behind. At 30+ it is read: Proven is ahead of the prices by z ≥ 2, Working is ahead, No edge is not. If faded is what backing the OTHER side of the same bets would have returned — not this row's ROI with the sign flipped, since both sides of a book are sold above fair, so fading an average rule loses that overround plus its fee; a large positive there means the rule is picking the wrong side, which is a different complaint from having no edge. v the close is how far the price moved toward the pick between the bet and the start, in cents, with t — the mean over its own standard error. It answers a different question and answers it sooner: a win record carries the outcome's own noise, so near an even price a real 2% edge needs thousands of settled bets to reach z 2, while the same closing prices read in tens. Called ahead or behind at t 2 over 20+ fresh closes; level is a real answer, not a missing one. Beating the close is not the same as making money, and nothing is promoted or retired on it alone. Production is entered by hand.

How a lane is replayedentries, exits, fees, control

A lane buys before the event starts, at a set time before it (1 hour before, and a 24-hour variant), at the ask on the first tight quote (spread 3c or less, 5c for the cheap-side lane). The standard exit is the split: half the stake rests at 2x the entry ask and half at 3x, each capped at 0.98, and a leg fills only when a trade prints at or above it; a leg not filled is held to Kalshi's settlement. Nothing is bought during the event. The start comes from the lane's own kickoff source where it has one, otherwise from the price path.

Other exits, from the same entry: a resting limit sell at +20%, +30%, +50% or +100% ROI after fees, each with no stop or a −50% stop. Those sells fill when the bid trades 1c through the limit inside a minute; a minute touching both the stop and the limit takes the stop. Fees: taker 0.07·p(1−p) on entry and on a stop, maker 0.0175·p(1−p) on a limit exit or a filled split leg.

The control is the same entry held to settlement. A variant is flagged for review at 10 settled events; promotion needs 30 AND a better ROI than its hold control. An event (one game, match or fight) is one outcome: several entries on one event move together, so they count once. z is clustered by night, never below the independent-trades z. Every lane's record starts fresh from its first replayed night and never inherits an older lane's rows; the split was added to the older lanes on 2026-10-27 by replaying their recorded entries, nothing edited or removed.

Each lane's card (split v hold, its other exits, its trades) is on its own page: tennis lanes on the Tennis page, NBA lanes on the NBA page, college football on the NFL page.

Method
Tipsters and rules name a side and are backed every time; models, books and exchanges state a probability and are backed only on a 3pp disagreement with the price. Polymarket US is the venue wherever it lists a contest; Kalshi is the venue for soccer and anything Polymarket US is missing, with soccer kickoffs taken from ESPN. A contest is logged only with a real book (spread ≤ 5¢), at the ask. Until 2026-09-13 the venue was polymarket.com; those bets still settle there but are not counted when a pair is judged. Quotes logged before the book rule on boxing, cricket and table tennis were voided (pre-gate: logged at an unverified Polymarket placeholder price). Pinnacle prices come through The Odds API: 1 of 1 allowed paid calls last run, 453 credits left until they reset on the 1st. A closing price counts only when taken within 60 minutes of the start (523 of 571 on the pairs still running). A positive ROI under 30 settled bets is not a finding.
