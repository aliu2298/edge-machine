Stamp of approval

A source earns the stamp only when every criterion holds, and it is re-judged every run, so it can be lost. The rules were fixed on 2026-09-12, before any source had met them, and they are the same for tipsters, models, books and exchanges: 50+ settled bets across 4+ different weeks (one weekend is one draw of the weather — on 2026-09-12 the tracked leagues drew 34% of the time against a normal ~26%); wins beat the price by z ≥ 2; ROI beats every blind rule on the same contests — back the favourite, back the underdog, back the draw — each a fixed rule that ignores the source, so beating all three means its choices added something; still profitable without its single biggest win; and profitable in both halves of its record. Watch means readable and ahead of the price but not yet through every gate; failing means readable and not ahead of the price at all; no read means under 30 settled bets.

Won v priced is the honest column while samples are small: each backed price is the market's own chance that the bet lands, so their sum is how many winners luck alone would have produced. Two wins from bets the market priced at 1.6 is noise, not an edge. Brier scores raw accuracy on every logged probability, bet or not — lower is better, 0.25 is a coin flip. It is blank for tipsters by design: naming a side states no probability, so there is nothing to calibrate. Compare on ROI, never on P/L — a tipster backs every game it calls while a model bets only where it disagrees with the price, so turnover differs by an order of magnitude. Polymarket cannot win its own table: its price is what everything else is measured against.

Method

Every source is logged before kick-off at the price available then, staked flat $100, and settled on the real result. Tipsters name a side and are backed every time; models and books state a probability and are backed only on a 3pp disagreement with the price.

Prices and settlement. Polymarket is the venue wherever it lists a contest, and settles it — but a Polymarket contest is only logged once it has a real book: a spread of 5¢ or less with at least $100 on it, booked at the ask. A just-listed market shows a midpoint near 50¢ with nothing behind it; before this rule, boxing bouts were logged at 51¢ that traded at 88¢ once money arrived. Boxing, cricket and table-tennis quotes logged before the rule were voided (pre-gate: logged at an unverified Polymarket placeholder price). Pinnacle prices come through The Odds API: 4 of ? allowed paid calls last run, 483 credits left until they reset on the 1st — each run is paced to its share of what remains.

Kalshi is the venue for soccer — Polymarket lists barely any soccer matches — and for any fight, match or game Polymarket is missing. On Kalshi a tip is backed at the ask, the price backing it would actually cost, and only where that book is tight (a spread of 10¢ or less). Soccer is three-way: a Draw tip wins on a draw, and a backed side loses to it and is never refunded.

What would falsify a source. A positive ROI under 30 settled bets is not a finding. Beating the price is the only test that counts — a high hit rate on heavy favourites is not an edge, it is just backing the favourite.
