Fights

Cheap side + resting take-profit.

  • Buy the cheap fighter about an hour before the fight starts, at the real ask.
  • Rest a split sell: half at 2x, half at 3x.
  • A fill counts only on a traded print in a minute candle, never the bid.
  • Every lane is shown next to the same entries held.

Sandbox, paper only, each lane on a fresh record from its first entry: review at 10 settled events (fights), decide at 30. The start is Kalshi's scheduled start for the fight.

ROI is after fees, the mean per entry (equal stake); split ROI counts entries whose two legs are resolved, hold ROI entries whose market has settled. A settled event is one Kalshi event whose entries have all settled: the rungs or words of one event move together, so it is one outcome, and the status counts settled events. Until a lane has a settled event its ROI reads pending: legs filled before anything settles are not a record. Entries count only when the ask was there: 10+ contracts on the book at entry, or 10+ traded at or through it within 60 minutes, and never under half the last trade; the rest are unverified asks, kept on the ledger and left out of the record.

Research (134 matches): 31 entries, 0 won, but 24 traded at 2x or more at some point. Split +53% (z 3.0) if brief in-fight spikes fill; -81% counting only hourly closes. This lane exists to measure whether minute-level spikes really fill, so every fill records the traded volume of its minute.

KXBOXING (boxing match winner) only. About one hour before a fight's scheduled start (Kalshi's milestone), the first minute in the next 15 with a spread of 5c or less and a YES ask of 0.03-0.15: buy that fighter YES at the ask. A split resting sell placed at entry: half at 2x, half at 3x the ask paid, each capped at 0.98. A leg fills only when a trade prints at or above its target (the minute candle's traded high, never the bid); an unfilled leg is held to settlement. HOLD is recorded on the same entries. Taker fee in, maker fee out. Every fill records the traded volume of its minute.
