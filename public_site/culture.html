Culture

Cheap side + resting take-profit.

  • Buy cheap contracts at the event's open, at the real ask.
  • Rest a split sell: half at 2x, half at 3x.
  • A fill counts only on a traded print.
  • Every lane is shown next to the same entries held.

Sandbox, paper only, each lane on a fresh record from its first entry: review at 10 settled events, decide at 30. An event is one outcome: the words of one mention market, or the rungs of one album ladder, move together, so they count once.
