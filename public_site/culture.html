Methodfour observation lanes · paper

Four observation lanes on Kalshi culture markets, registered 2026-10-16 to watch, not to trade. Paper entries at the ask with Kalshi's 7% fee, settled on Kalshi's own result. Nothing here is in Production.

Measured before the build on 4,558 settled culture markets (Aug 11 – Oct 16): 24h before the close about 70% sit under 5c or over 95c, so the favourite band held 8 events in two months (7-1, z +0.94) and the 90-97c end 57 (98.2% won, z +1.20).
