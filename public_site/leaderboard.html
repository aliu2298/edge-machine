How the spike board is measured

Contract. One cheap entry from a ledger the Edge Machine already keeps: the NBA moneyline underdog, team total and win margin trackers, spreads (including the Comeback line), the tennis and college football chaos lanes, the over 3.5 cheap NO bids, and the WTI, gas, album, mentions and fight lanes. Only entries whose ask was real count: the lane's own ask check, or the same test (10+ contracts traded at or under the ask in the hour after the entry) on a tracker row. Dead fills and open markets are left out.

Reached 2x / 3x. The share of contracts whose traded price hit twice (three times) the entry. Fair is 50% (33%). Split ROI is half sold at 2x and half at 3x on trade prints, after fees; hold ROI keeps the contract to the end. Traded at 2x+ is the median contracts that changed hands at 2x or better, where the source records it.

Score. Reached-2x minus 50%, shrunk toward the average of that sport and market type with a prior worth 20 contracts, so 3 for 3 cannot top the board. A name ranks only with 15+ verified contracts across 5+ events; the rest are Watching.

Holds up? Reached-2x in the name's older half of events v its newer half; ✅ when both beat fair.

Names. NBA by the team in the ticker; a match contract (over 3.5) counts for both teams and is marked match; tennis and college football by the code in the ticker (Kalshi does not give a name in the ledgers); commodities by WTI and AAA gas; Trump for the mentions lanes; the fighter for fights. No cheap-side crypto ledger exists yet, so no crypto name can appear.
