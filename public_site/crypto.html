The rule under test

Registered 2026-10-05, before it logged anything, and registered expecting to find nothing. Every number below was fixed in advance.
EntryYes ask 0.70–0.80, both ends inclusive
Window2–3.5 hours before the 17:00 Eastern close — about a three-hour hold, settling the same afternoon. Matched in Eastern, not in UTC: that close is 21:00Z under daylight time and 22:00Z under standard time, and a fixed UTC hour would have silently stopped the lane at the November change. The hourly closes on the same series are a different contract and are not this bet.
When it readsA launchd timer on the always-on Mac dispatches the tracker at 13:15 local Central, permanently 2.83h before the close because Central and Eastern shift on the same date. GitHub’s cron cannot hold a 90-minute window: measured over 18 days it ran 4.2 times a day against 8 scheduled, landing inside the window on 10 of 18.
Which rungThe lowest-priced rung in band, one per coin. A ladder offers several at once, so the choice is fixed in advance rather than after seeing results.
Why one per coinRungs are nested, not exclusive — “$84,750 or above” and “$84,500 or above” settle on one move. Counting both is one bet counted twice, the defect that once read the spot baseline at z +10.40 against a true +0.24.
Liquidity floorTwo-sided quote, spread ≤ 3c, ask size ≥ 25. One coin quoted a single price across fourteen consecutive strikes, which is a stale book rather than fourteen prices.
Coins liveKXBTCD, KXETHD, KXHYPED, KXSOLD, KXXRPD
Listed, no open marketBTCD, ETHD, KXLINKD, KXNEAR, KXNEARD, KXXLM, KXXLMD, KXXRP, KXZECD
Judged againstBacking every in-band rung in the window, so the lane only counts if picking the lowest beats the band itself.
Read at30 independent outcomes — and an outcome is a day, so about six weeks.
What it has to beat. At the taker fee the effective price at mid-band is 0.7632, so the band must beat its own price by about 1.3pp just to break even — roughly the whole size of the favourite-longshot bias anywhere this Sandbox has measured it. The one direct prior is against it: the spot baseline’s own 0.70–0.80 cell ran −1.9pp at z −0.17 on 10 bets over 8 days.

Why a day is one bet

Both crypto pairs are scored per market-day, not per bet, and that is measured rather than assumed. Over 90 days of daily closes for the five live coins:
Mean pairwise return correlation+0.757
Mean sign agreement77.8%
Days all five move the same way54.4%
Variance inflation (k=5)4.03
Independent draws in a day1.24, not 5
z overstatement if keyed per coin2.01×
A day of “or above” rungs wins or loses together because the coins do, so the day is the unit. Keying per coin would overstate the evidence by about double, in exactly the direction that makes a dead lane look alive — which is how the spot baseline once read z +10.40 against a true +0.24.

Back the YES on a Kalshi coin-directional rung priced 0.70-0.80 inclusive, entered 2-3.5 hours before the 17:00 ET (21:00Z) close, so the bet is held about three hours and settles the same afternoon. ONE bet per coin per close, the LOWEST-priced rung in band — a ladder offers several at once (BTC offered 0.73 and 0.80 on 2026-10-05, HYPE offered fourteen), so the choice is fixed in advance instead of after seeing results. One per coin because rungs are NESTED, not exclusive: '$84,750 or above' and '$84,500 or above' settle on one move, and counting both is the defect that once inflated the spot baseline to z +10.40 against a true +0.24. FIVE coins are live (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, HYPE); four more are listed with no open event. WHAT IT MUST BEAT: the effective price at mid-band is 0.7632 after the taker fee, so the band has to beat its own price by about 1.3pp just to break even, which is roughly the whole size of the favourite-longshot bias anywhere this Sandbox has measured it — and the one direct prior is against it, the spot baseline's own 0.70-0.80 cell running -1.9pp at z -0.17 on 10 bets over 8 days. AN OUTCOME IS A DAY, NOT A BET: across 90 days of daily closes the five coins' returns correlate +0.757 on average, agree in sign 77.8% of the time, and all five move together on 54.4% of days, so a day holds 1.24 independent draws and not five. Judged against backing every in-band rung in the window, so the lane only counts if picking the lowest beats the band itself. Read at 30 independent outcomes, about six weeks. A rung also needs a two-sided quote, a spread of 3c or less and 25 contracts on the ask: HYPE quoted one price on fourteen consecutive strikes, which is a stale book, not a signal.
