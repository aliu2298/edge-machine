What these runs are, and what the rarity chip means

All teams browses every tracked side's current runs — a complete league browser, not a highlight reel, so a team with nothing going still appears.

On fire looks back further than the 6-game form window to catch genuinely long runs. Measured, long runs do not extend more often than a shuffled fixture list would produce — see the Record page.

Every run carries a rarity chip: the share of tracked teams currently on a run that long. When that share is high the pattern is ordinary, and the chip says so.

Form is measured across all tracked competitions (last 6 games, minimum 3 played here). Looking for a fixture to act on? The Leads page pairs these runs into upcoming matches.
