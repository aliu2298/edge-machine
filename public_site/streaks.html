A run only counts when the opponent is soft in the same place — how this works

A streak on its own is not an edge; plenty of good sides score freely. What is shown here is the confluence: one team's run meeting the other's matching weakness in a fixture not yet played.

Every lead carries a rarity chip — the share of tracked teams currently on a run that long. When that share is high the pattern is ordinary, and the chip says so. Leads are sorted rarest first, not longest.

Form is measured across all tracked competitions (last 6 games, minimum 5 played), because form does not reset when a side walks into a European tie.

Confluences are genuinely rare — a whole league can have none on a given day. All teams on a run browses the raw runs instead. Leads to look at: not picks, and not betting advice.
