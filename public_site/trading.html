Read this first: a headline is not an edge

Big-cap news is priced within seconds to minutes. A public headline read later is NOT an edge, so a news call counts only from the moment it was logged, at the price at that moment. How the record is kept
The five families were pre-registered on 2026-11-07, before the first prediction, in a committed file. The tests pin that file, so a family can never be edited after the fact; a change is a new family with a fresh record. A prediction counts only if it was committed before its entry. Its entry is the first honest fill after it was logged. Honest fill: an Up call buys at the ask and a Down call sells at the bid. The fill counts only when a public trade prints at or through that quote within 5 minutes. Otherwise it retries every 5 minutes for 30 minutes and is then recorded as no fill. The exit uses the same rule. At 1x, no leverage, minus the 0.12% taker fee on each side. Funding is not charged. A call logged while the perp is closed enters at the reopen. Baselines, over the identical windows: always-Up on the same stock, a random side fixed when the call was logged, and the SPY perp Up. The z column is the weakest of the three paired z-scores. Review at 10 settled, decide at 30: paying needs z ≥ 2 against every baseline.

How the record is kept
Paper · never places an order · not in Production. QQQ opening-range breakout, four pre-registered variants, one row each per trading day, no stop, one position. Reviewed at 10+ settled days per variant. The range is the high and low of the 1-minute bars in the variant's window (A 9:30–10:00 ET, B 9:30–9:35, C 9:25–9:30 premarket). The signal is the first minute, before 11:00 ET, that closes above the high (long) or below the low (short); entry is the next minute's open and exit the close 15 or 30 minutes later. No signal by 11:00 is no break; market holidays and half-days are skipped. Gross is open to close on SIP bars, read once they are 15 minutes old, and every decision uses only bars closed by that minute. Net buys at the ask and sells at the bid from the quote taken at the start of the entry and exit minutes: IEX top of book (this data plan refuses the SIP quote), and no commission. Days counts settled plus no-break days; t is on net when quotes exist, else gross. The backtest on 807 days, done the same way, was flat to negative; this lane watches it live.

Run by market_track.py and market_exec.py in edge-machine, on edge-vps (edge-market.timer, weekdays 16:10 ET). The table is each rule's paper record since its registration, every signal at the bar's open; it is not the Alpaca account.

How the cost is measured

From 2026-10-29 every rule is judged at the mean adverse gap between each Alpaca paper fill and the open the record used, positive on both sides. It rolls: the fill check reads the last 120 days of orders every weekday. A rule with 30+ fills of its own is charged its own mean; one with fewer is charged the mean over all fills. It is more than slippage: the record's open is IEX's first print, and an order fills in the opening auction.

The ledger still stores each trade at 5bp a side; the page charges the measured cost when it judges. Entry and exit rules are unchanged.

Twelve earlier rules (retired 2026-10-04 and 2026-10-20) are off the board; their trades stay in the ledger.

How the record is kept
Each rule is pre-registered: its thresholds and the reason for them are fixed before it logs a trade. A trade is logged only from bars that closed BEFORE it, and enters at the next bar's open — never the signal bar's close. Costs are charged on entry and exit, at the cost the Alpaca fills measured (above the table); the ledger itself stores each trade at the 5bp a side first assumed. Judged per entry day, because names bought the same morning rise and fall together, and against SPY over the identical days: beating a rising market is not an edge. Read at 30+ entry days (Working, No edge or Proven); from 10 it is Collecting n/30 with its lean, Promising or Behind; under 10 a rule is only Too early.
