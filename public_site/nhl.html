Retired 2026-11-11: No edge in two full NHL studies (Oct 30, Nov 8): moneyline, totals and puck line priced fairly. Paused until something new comes up.

Method and researchhow the lanes are read · the study's verdicts

Both lanes are read after the final horn from Kalshi's 1-minute candles and trade prints and ESPN's play-by-play, so an entry appears once its game has settled. Nothing is placed: paper only.

They come from a full breakdown of the Kalshi NHL markets in which nothing passed the multiple-test bar; these two cells pointed the same way on a holdout and are tested forward, frozen.

Research: the Kalshi NHL markets2026-27 discovery (Oct 6 to Nov 7) and a 2025-26 holdout (Mar 6 to Apr 17)

Every NHL market at five moments (an hour before the start, three minutes after a goal, the two intermissions, the late 3rd with the score unchanged), both sides of the price, six pre-match filters registered before the results were read.
