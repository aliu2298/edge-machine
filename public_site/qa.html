Production-ready is the full stamp applied to the fresh QA record — 50+ bets spanning 28+ days, z ≥ 2, beats every blind rule, still profitable without its biggest win and in both halves — plus buying below the closing price on average, measured on 30+ closing prices covering at least 50% of the bets, and staying profitable after the taker fee (Polymarket US 0.06·p·(1−p), Kalshi 0.07), and every bet must be a standard exchange market the Production feed can publish — today a soccer side (home or away) on a Kalshi game market, or Yes on a Kalshi over-1.5 or team-goals market; no draws and no other sport yet. The gate is checked every run, so a pair is marked ready only once it has held for 7 days, and the mark is withdrawn the first run it fails. A pair goes back to the Sandbox after 30 fresh bets if it is behind the price, not beating every blind rule, or behind the closing price — or, at any count, after 21 days without a new bet — and must re-qualify on bets logged after the demotion. Baselines are benchmarks and are never promoted.
