NFL team total · OVER 35-45c · Kalshi NFL team total · over · registered 2026-10-21 · paper · own ledger

Kalshi NFL team totals (KXNFLTEAMTOTAL). Per team per game, back YES (over) on the CHEAPEST rung of that team's ladder whose YES ask is ≥0.35 and <0.45; a tie goes to the lower ticker. Entry 45-75 minutes before the ESPN kickoff, one frozen entry per team per game. Only a two-sided YES quote with spread ≤5c counts, no depth floor. Settled on Kalshi's official result after the 7% taker fee; stats clustered by NFL week. Blind baseline: the YES on every in-window team-total rung with the same quote. Paper, Sandbox only.

Methodvenue note

NFL picks are match winners, priced on Polymarket US or Kalshi. Every pick is logged before kickoff at the price available then, settled on the real result at a flat $100 stake, and judged after fees against the price it paid.

ESPN FPI was paused on 2026-09-25 with every NFL lane and resumed by the owner on 2026-10-16; its record counts from its 2026-09-21 reset.
