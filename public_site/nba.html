Win margin · YES split (v4) · Win margin · YES

Entry: buy YES on a 1-5, 6-10 or 11-20 win margin bucket (either team) at the ask, 10-20c, an hour before the tip; skip a game whose favourite's pregame win share is 70% or more.

Exit: rest half at 2x and half at 2.5x of the ask, filled only by prints strictly through the target from the tip on; the rest is held to settlement. Production: traded live, version 4 from the night of 2026-11-08.

Win margin tracker

Observation only — no buying or selling rule · paper · no orders · since 2026-10-20 · 695 contracts · 160 games since 2026-10-20

How the tracker is readentries, reach, fair rates

Every game's bucket contracts (both teams: 1-5, 6-10, 11-20, 21+) are read at the ask one hour before the tip, then followed through every trade print. Only contracts with an entry ask of 5-35c and a spread of 5c or less count. The tip is the Kalshi game market's time minus 3 hours.

Reached means a trade at or after the tip printed at 2, 3 or 5 times the entry ask (a target above 99c is left out of that rate). At fair prices a contract reaches 2x about 50% of the time, 3x 33% and 5x 20%. Held 10+ min is the share that stayed at 2x or more for ten minutes or longer. Min to 2x, Min above 2x and Contracts at 2x+ are medians over the contracts that reached 2x. Won minus ask is the share that settled yes less the average ask paid.

How these lanes are readone place for the rules that apply to every card
  • Both lanes are in Production: traded live. The record on this page is paper that mirrors what the live orders can do, replayed from Kalshi's trade prints and 1-minute candles.
  • A buy is at the real ask before the tip (or a resting bid after it); a sell fills only on a traded print.
  • The split sells half at 2x and half at 3x of the price paid. "Hold" is the same entries kept to settlement.
  • The outcome is the game: one game is one event however many entries it holds. z is clustered by night.
  • Review at 10 settled events, decide at 30. Maker fees on resting orders, taker fees on a market buy.
  • An entry counts only when its ask was real: 10+ contracts traded at or through it within an hour, and not under half the last trade. The rest are listed as unverified ask and leave the record.
  • An in-sample row replays the days a rule was chosen on. It is not evidence and is never a headline.

Every cheap-side lane is also listed in the Sandbox's Chaos index.

The board: 1Q, 1H and FT are expected combined points for both teams, from each team's last-5 scoring and allowing rates. A finished game is graded against its actual combined total, and the error shown is actual minus expected as stored in the pace file. These are research expectations, not betting lines or recommendations.
