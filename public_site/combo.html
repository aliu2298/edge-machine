Pre-registration

Crypto No with S&P No, one two-leg paper combo a trading day. Both legs are chosen at the minute the crypto No lane logs its pick: the crypto pick, and the cheapest S&P 16:00 ET rung with a No ask of 90c to 98c, a spread of 3c or less and 25 or more contracts at that minute. Priced at 0.994 x 1.03 x the product of the two No asks, plus the taker fee. The idea came from 19 days to Nov 19 2026 on which the two legs were priced at different times (17 landed, +23.1%); at one moment only 8 or 9 of those days could be formed (+5% to +30%). None of that counts: the row starts at zero. Read at 30 settled combos against the same legs as singles; retire if the combo is at or below them.

A combo leg is bought at the ask, at one minute. A lane that rests a bid or sells to a target cannot be a leg, because the combo has no resting order and cannot be sold early.
